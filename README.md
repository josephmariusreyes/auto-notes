🚗 Auto Notes
Auto Notes is a personal vehicle maintenance tracker that helps you stay on top of your car's preventive maintenance schedule (PMS) — and never miss a service again.

✨ Features
📋 Maintenance History Tracking — Log and organize every PMS session you've done on your vehicle, keeping a complete service record at your fingertips.

📤 Export Maintenance History — Export your full maintenance records for backup, sharing with mechanics, or keeping for resale documentation.

🤖 AI-Powered Maintenance Tips — Get personalized maintenance suggestions based on your vehicle's year, make, and model, so you know exactly what to look out for.

📍 Nearby Auto Shop Suggestions — Find auto shops in your area when you need professional service, right from the app.

🛠️ Why Auto Notes?
Keeping track of vehicle maintenance is easy to neglect — but missed PMS can lead to costly repairs. Auto Notes makes it simple to record what's been done, get smart recommendations for what's next, and find help nearby when you need it.
